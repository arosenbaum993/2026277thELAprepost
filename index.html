Start here

Four steps from booklet to instructional groups.

1 · Print the booklets

Open a form below and print to PDF — Letter, default margins, background graphics on.

2 · Administer Form A

First three weeks of school. Two 60-minute sessions. Scripts and accommodations are in the administration guide.

3 · Score and enter

Item-level, not totals. Enter 0 for a wrong answer — a blank means "not tested".

4 · Read the groups

The reporting workbook names each student's weakest reporting category and builds the groups for you.

The forms

Item n on both forms shares standard, expectation, achievement level, DOK, item type, and point value. Only the passages and answer positions differ — that is what makes a pre → post difference mean growth rather than a difference in difficulty.

What this instrument does not do

It does not predict Georgia Milestones scores. Cut scores were set by content review, not by a state standard-setting panel. Achievement levels here are instructional triage.

Category scores rest on as few as 6 points. Use them to form groups; confirm against student work before acting on an individual.

It measures no listening, speaking, collaboration, or presentation. Roughly a quarter of the Grade 7 expectations need classroom performance tasks. The reporting workbook names every one.

Form equivalence is by construction, not yet by data. Three local checks establish it statistically; the workbook computes them.
