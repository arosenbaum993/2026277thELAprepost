These gaps are real and the reporting workbook does not pretend otherwise. The Standards Coverage sheet lists every unassessed expectation so that a teacher planning from this data knows what the data is silent about. Roughly one-quarter of the Grade 7 expectations — largely the productive, multimodal, research, and oral-language ones — require classroom performance tasks. A pre/post booklet is a partial picture by design.
